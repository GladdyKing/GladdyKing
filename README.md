## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm **Davison Gladson**, also known online as **Gladdy King**. I'm a junior programmer from Zimbabwe who builds WhatsApp bots, automation tools and mobile-first projects. Most of my development happens on my phone using Termux, a full Linux environment on Android.

I got into programming by building bots for fun, and it grew into a real passion for automation: making software do the boring work so people don't have to. I like turning ideas into working tools, shipping them, and learning from the people who use and fork them.

- 🔭 I'm building **bots, automation tools and an Android voice assistant**
- 🌱 I'm learning **full-stack development** and getting stronger in **Node.js and Python**
- 🤝 I'm open to **collaboration, freelance bot work and junior developer opportunities**
- 🎥 I share tech content on my **YouTube channel, GLADDYTECH**
- 💬 Ask me about **WhatsApp bots, Baileys, Termux and automation**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I write real code from a phone, no laptop needed
